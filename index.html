01 / CONCEPT

Zero external models.
Zero heavy textures.

Most 3D websites download massive pre-made model files that slow down the browser. UNIT 01 takes a purer engineering path: every ceramic curve, glass visor, joint ring, and metallic surface is generated mathematically in code the moment you arrive.

Lit by a custom-synthesized studio environment and driven by layered spring physics, the sentinel responds naturally to your presence while keeping the entire page ultra-lightweight.
